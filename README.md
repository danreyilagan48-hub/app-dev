# My Favorite Movie: Inception (2010)

**Inception** is a mind-bending sci-fi heist thriller written and directed by *Christopher Nolan*. The film explores the concept of entering people's dreams to steal—or plant—information.

## Core Plot Summary
The story follows Dom Cobb, a professional thief who steals corporate secrets through dream-sharing technology. He is offered a chance to have his criminal history erased in exchange for performing **inception**: implanting another person's idea into a target's subconscious.

## Main Characters & Cast
* **Dom Cobb** – Played by Leonardo DiCaprio
* **Arthur** – Played by Joseph Gordon-Levitt
* **Ariadne** – Played by Elliot Page
* **Eames** – Played by Tom Hardy

## Key Elements of the Dream World
1. **The Architect:** The person who designs the structural layout of the dream landscapes.
2. **The Totem:** A small, personal object used to verify if a person is in their own reality or someone else's dream.
3. **The Kick:** A sensation of falling that jolts a dreamer awake from a deeper dream layer.

> "An idea is like a virus. Resilient. Highly contagious. And the smallest seed of an idea can grow. It can grow to define or destroy you."
> — *Dom Cobb*
